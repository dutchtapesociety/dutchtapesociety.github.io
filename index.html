How a fix works

A new fix starts every two weeks and runs for 30 days. Week one: we search, check every case and send a short, friendly note to whoever can repair it. Weeks two to four: we follow up. On day 30 we close the fix with a final score. After that the theme goes into maintenance: once a month we recheck the known pages and catch new ones.

Elke twee weken start een fix, die 30 dagen loopt: week 1 zoeken en melden, daarna opvolgen. Op dag 30 sluiten we af met een eindscore; daarna een maandelijkse controle.

FIX #003 · STARTED 11 NOV 2026searching

Accessibility: barriers on municipal websites that one line can remove

We are working through all Dutch municipal websites, looking for barriers that block people and can be fixed in a single line. First check: can you zoom in on a phone? 82 homepages checked so far; one blocked pinch-zoom on every page. It has been reported to the contact point from its accessibility statement.

FIX #001 · STARTED 10 NOV 20263 of 37 fixed

The wrong number: outdated crisis lines in the Netherlands

Since October 2025 the Dutch suicide prevention line is free on 113 and 0800-0113. Many pages still listed the old paid 0900 number, and one even told readers the short number did not work. That page has since been corrected. A second search round turned up 21 more pages, several of which had copied the same outdated sentence word for word.

day 2 of 30closes 10 Dec

Het verkeerde nummer: 37 pagina's gevonden, 3 al aangepast, 2 toegezegd.

FIX #002 · STARTED 11 NOV 2026reported

Phishing clean-up: a fake bank login and a fake parcel company

We checked recently registered and recently scanned sites using the names of Dutch banks, DigiD and parcel services. Two were live and fake: a copied Rabobank login page and a fake "PostNL" freight company. Both were reported to the hosting company, the bank and Google Safe Browsing.

House rules

  1. Check every case. Each page is opened and read one by one, with AI assistance, before it goes on the list.
  2. Fix, don't shame. Owners hear from us first, and we never name them here.
  3. Never pretend to be someone else. We're a small volunteer project that uses AI tools, and we say so.
  4. No names, no credit. The fix is the point.
