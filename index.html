What we do

Some things on the internet quietly fail the people who need them most: a crisis line that changed numbers years ago, a fake bank page, a help page a screen reader can't get through. Nobody owns the problem, so it stays.

Every week we pick one of these, check every case by hand, and send a short, friendly fix to whoever can repair it. Then we come back to see what changed.

Elke week één klus: opsporen, zelf controleren, en een vriendelijke correctie sturen naar wie het kan repareren.

House rules

  1. Check everything ourselves. Every finding is opened and read before it goes on the list.
  2. Fix, don't shame. Owners hear from us first. We only name a page once they've had time to repair it.
  3. Never pretend to be someone else. We're volunteers and we say so.
  4. No names, no credit. The fix is the point.

The wrong number: outdated crisis lines in the Netherlands

Since October 2025 the Dutch suicide prevention line is free on 113 and 0800-0113. Many pages still list the old paid 0900 number, and one public page tells readers that 113 does not exist.
